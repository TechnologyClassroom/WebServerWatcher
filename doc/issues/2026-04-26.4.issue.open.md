Testing this is kind of tricky because you do not want to test in production. Old log files have timestamps that are older than 4 seconds ago.

While developing, I was able to test with a log streamed to a file. In one `tmux` pane, I ran this command:

`ssh root@www.server.com 'tail -f /var/log/access.log' | tee test/prod.log`

In another `tmux` pane, I ran the script. In another `tmux` pane, I watched the logs.

It seemed to work.
